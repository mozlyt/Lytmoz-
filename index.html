Questions

What people ask before they start.

What does Lytmoz do?

Lytmoz builds websites and business systems for ideas and running businesses in Ghana and Nigeria: your website, your team, subscriptions, payments and one HQ dashboard, all working together.

I only have an idea. Can Lytmoz help?

Yes. Book a free consultation and audit. We help you shape the idea into a clear offer, then build the website and system around it so it is ready to grow from day one.

How much does a website or system cost?

A premium website starts at $600 setup plus $30 a month for care. A full business system starts at $2,500 plus $100 a month. You can pay in US dollars or the equivalent in cedis or naira, and a 50% deposit starts the work.

Do you work with clients outside Ghana?

Yes. Lytmoz serves clients anywhere in the world. Consultations happen on WhatsApp or video call, and you follow every step in your client portal.

How long does it take?

A Launch website usually takes two to three weeks once the content is agreed. Larger systems take longer, and you can see every stage live in your client portal.

What happens after my system goes live?

Monthly care keeps it running: updates, fixes, new features and reports. You are never left alone with a system you cannot manage.
