How billing works

  1. Agree the work. After your consultation we send a proposal with the price and timeline.
  2. Get your invoice. It appears here, usually a deposit to start, then the balance at launch.
  3. Pay your way. Online by card or Mobile Money, or by a direct Mobile Money or bank transfer. You can pay part of an invoice, such as your deposit.
  4. Online payments update at once. For a direct transfer, tap Submit payment reference on the invoice and add the transaction ID so we can match it.
  5. We verify transfers. Once the money is in, your balance updates and your receipt is saved in Payment history.

Help & support

Quick answers and ways to reach us.

How do I follow my project?

Open My projects. You will see the stage, latest updates and a preview link when it is ready.

How do I send my logo, photos or text?

Go to Files & content and upload them. Up to 25 MB per file.

How do I pay an invoice?

Open Billing and tap Pay on the invoice. Pay online by card or Mobile Money, or send a transfer and submit the transaction ID. You can pay a deposit first and the balance later.

Can my staff log in too?

Yes. In My team, create your business workspace, then add their emails after they create a free account. You can then assign them work and discuss it there.
