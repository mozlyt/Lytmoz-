Questions

What people ask before they start.

What does Lytmoz do?

Lytmoz helps you move from an idea to something that works. We listen, work out what your idea or business needs, bring in the right professionals, and build it: websites, apps and business systems with your team, subscriptions, payments and one HQ dashboard working together.

I only have an idea. Can Lytmoz help?

Yes. You do not need to have everything figured out. Book a free consultation: we listen, help you shape the idea into a clear offer, then build the website and system around it so it is ready to grow from day one.

How is a consultation done?

It starts with a free conversation where a professional listens to your idea, needs and challenges. We then assess what your situation requires, bring in specialists where the matter calls for them, and agree a way forward with you. If you choose to go ahead, Lytmoz coordinates and delivers the work.

Does Lytmoz give legal advice?

Legal, tax and financial advice is given by qualified, independent professionals, not by Lytmoz itself. When your matter needs it, for example business registration, contracts, partnerships or legal structure, we bring a lawyer into your consultation. Any specialist fee is agreed with you before that work starts.

How much does a website or system cost?

A premium website starts at $600 setup plus $30 a month for care. A full business system starts at $2,500 plus $100 a month. You can pay in US dollars or the equivalent in cedis or naira, and a 50% deposit starts the work.

Do you work with clients outside Ghana?

Yes. Lytmoz serves clients anywhere in the world. Consultations happen on WhatsApp or video call, and you follow every step in your client portal.

How long does it take?

A Launch website usually takes two to three weeks once the content is agreed. Larger systems take longer, and you can see every stage live in your client portal.

What happens after my system goes live?

Monthly care keeps it running: updates, fixes, new features and reports. You are never left alone with a system you cannot manage.
