Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information Lytmoz collects when you visit lytmoz.com or use the Lytmoz client portal, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, business name and what you tell us about your idea or project.
  • Files, messages and requests you send through the client portal.
  • Billing details you enter, such as the name and address on your invoices. Card and Mobile Money payments are handled by Paystack. We do not see or keep your card number.
  • Basic technical information needed to keep you signed in and the site working.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, run your consultation and deliver the work you agree with us.
  • To send invoices and receipts and to confirm payments.
  • To keep your account secure.

We never sell your details.

Messages and online status

The client portal has a Messages feature for chatting with Lytmoz and with other clients.

  • You choose whether other clients can see you. Go to More, then Privacy & security, and switch "Show me in Messages" on or off. It is off until you turn it on.
  • When it is off, other clients cannot find you or start a chat with you, and they do not see when you are online. You can still message Lytmoz.
  • Conversations between clients are shown only to the two people in them. Lytmoz HQ does not show them to our team.
  • You can block anyone. Neither of you can then send messages in that chat.

Who we share it with

Only with the services that run Lytmoz (hosting, database, email alerts and Paystack for payments) and with professionals you ask us to bring into your work, such as a lawyer or accountant. They may use your information only for that purpose.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and project records while you are a client and for as long as the law requires for invoices and payments. You can ask us to close your account and delete your personal details at any time from the client portal.

Your choices

  • See and update your details in the client portal.
  • Turn your visibility in Messages on or off at any time.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to info@lytmoz.com or message us on WhatsApp.
